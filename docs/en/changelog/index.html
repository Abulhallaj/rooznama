CHANGELOG

Every release,
a little more capable.

A summary of Rooznama releases. See the repository CHANGELOG for the complete technical history.

2 Azar 1405 · 23 November 2026 · v0.10.0

In-app help

  • Added searchable, expandable help topics in Persian and English.
  • Help is available from navigation, the tray menu or the F1 key.
  • Added tests for help markup, topics and search.
30 Aban 1405 · 21 November 2026 · v0.9.0

Fixed calendar occasions and main calendar improvements

  • Added fixed Hijri and Gregorian occasion rules alongside Jalali rules.
  • Added Iranian-rule week numbers, with a Settings option to turn them off.
  • Added arrow-key day navigation and PageUp/PageDown month navigation.
  • Added a distinct holiday background in the main calendar.
  • Separated the main calendar control from the tray flyout.
29 Aban 1405 · 20 November 2026 · v0.8.2

Date converter improvement

  • The Hijri date in the converter is now selected with a date picker, aligned with the Shamsi and Gregorian pickers.
20 Aban 1405 · 11 November 2026 · v0.8.0

Hijri calendar support

  • Hijri dates added to tooltips, the main calendar and day details.
  • Choose between Tehran University Calendar Center data and Umm al-Qura.
  • Convert between Shamsi, Gregorian and Hijri dates.
12 Aban 1405 · 3 November 2026 · v0.7.0

Holidays and observances

  • Bundled official holidays and observances for Jalali years 1383–1405, available offline.
