FAQ

A few answers,
before you begin.

If you cannot find what you need, open an issue or discussion in the Rooznama GitHub repository.

Is Rooznama free?

Yes. Rooznama is free and open source under the MIT License. There are no plans for a paid or commercial edition.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The calendar, date converter and bundled observances work offline. Internet access is used only to check for updates.

Does Rooznama collect any information?

No. Rooznama collects no personal data or usage statistics. Settings and logs stay on your device.

How can I disable automatic update checks?

Turn off the automatic update-check option in the app settings. You can still check manually whenever you choose.

What is the difference between installer and portable?

The installer sets up the app for your Windows user account. The portable ZIP needs no installation and stores settings and logs in a data folder beside the executable.

Should I download x64 or x86?

Choose x64 for 64-bit Windows and x86 for 32-bit Windows.

Does the Hijri date always match Iran's official calendar?

The Hijri source is selectable in Settings. Official Tehran University Calendar Center data is used for covered years; Umm al-Qura is used when official data does not cover a date, so a one-day difference may occur.

Why are some observances marked approximate?

For years without bundled official Hijri data, some observances are calculated from fixed rules and may be approximate.
