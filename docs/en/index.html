A Shamsi calendar for Windows

Your date,
always within reach.

Rooznama brings the Persian (Jalali) calendar to the part of Windows you visit every day: the system tray.

See the Shamsi day on the tray icon, open the calendar with a click, and keep Shamsi, Gregorian and Hijri dates close at hand.
