ROOZNAMA FEATURES

The calendars you need,
at a glance.

From the date beside your clock to a three-calendar converter, Rooznama is designed for everyday access.

System tray date

Show the Shamsi day number on the taskbar icon, with Shamsi and Gregorian details in the tooltip.

Calendar flyout

A compact calendar beside the taskbar, with day, month and year views.

Main calendar

A larger monthly view with Gregorian and Hijri dates in day cells, week numbers and keyboard navigation.

Three-calendar converter

Convert between Shamsi, Gregorian and Hijri dates. The Hijri source follows the setting selected in the app.

Holidays & observances

Bundled official observances for Jalali years 1383–1405, plus fixed calendar rules to fill later years. Rule-based Hijri dates may be approximate.

Themes & languages

Light, dark or Windows-matched theme; Persian and English interface.

In-app help

Searchable topic-based help, available from the menu or with F1.

Start with Windows

Optionally launch Rooznama when you sign in to Windows.
